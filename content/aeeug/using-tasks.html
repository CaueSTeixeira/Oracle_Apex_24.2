5 Using Tasks

When configured in Oracle APEX, tasks allow users to approve, reject, or complete items.

  • About Tasks
    You can use tasks to approve, reject, or complete items. Depending on your role, you may be able to interact with tasks by leaving comments, submitting additional information, or updating due dates.
  • Working with Tasks
    Your ability to update tasks depends on the role you have for the task, the state the task is in, and the way the application is configured. Depending on the way the application is configured, some features may be unavailable.
  • About Unified Task Lists
    Unified Task Lists allow you to see a summary of your tasks.
  • Troubleshooting Tasks
    Common questions and solutions for tasks.
