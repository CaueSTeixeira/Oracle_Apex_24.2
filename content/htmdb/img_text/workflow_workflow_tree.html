Screenshot of the Workflow Designer Tree, which depicts the workflow in tree form. Under the Workflows heading, there is one workflow called Sample Workflow. The workflow is depicted with a green background, and an icon of a person's silhouette with a wrench.

The second level in the hierarchy is a workflow version , titled New [Dev]. The workflow version name is in bold font, and preceded by an icon depicting a hierarchical flow chart.

The third level in the hierarchy has three subheadings: Activities, Participants, and Variables. Under Activities, there are three available activities (Start, Activity, End), each of which can be expanded. Under Participants, there is one entry for Workflow Owner, and one entry for Workflow Administrator. Variables is at the botton of the screenshot, and does not have anything underneath it.
