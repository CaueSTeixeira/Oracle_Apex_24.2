5.20.1 Accessing Workspace Utilities

Access Workspace Utilities by clicking the Workspace Utilities icon in the center of the {Conref}App Builder home page.

To access Workspace Utilities:

  1. Sign in to Oracle APEX.
  2. On the Workspace home page, click the App Builder icon.

    The App Builder home page appears.

  3. On the App Builder home pack, click the Workspace Utilities icon.
    The Workspace Utilities page appears.
