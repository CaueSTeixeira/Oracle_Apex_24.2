21.1.5 About Configuring Workspace Purge Settings

Save storage space and improve system performance by purging inactive workspaces.

Inactive workspaces consume valuable storage space and degrade system performance. By enabling Workspace Purge Settings, administrators can configure Oracle APEX to purge inactive workspaces.
