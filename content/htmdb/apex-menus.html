2.4.1 APEX Menus

Use the APEX menus to access to the following development tools: App Builder, SQL Workshop, Team Development, and the Gallery.

Each menu offers quick access to key development tools, components, and functions. In the following example, the App Builder menu is selected and displays the menu options Create, Import, Export, and Workspace Utilities.
