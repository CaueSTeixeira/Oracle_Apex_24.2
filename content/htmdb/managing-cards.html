8.5 Managing Cards

A cards page features an orderly layout of information tiles. Developers choose between three layouts: Grid, Float, or Horizontal (Row).

A cards report region declaratively supports customizations of layout and appearance and the inclusion of icons, badges, media, and actions. Use cards to embed and share media sourced from a BLOB column, URL or video in iFrame.
